Terms, returns and privacy

Returns and the right to change your mind

You have 14 days from the day you receive an order to change your mind, without giving a reason. Email us within that time, then send the piece back within 14 days of telling us.

Returns are free: we pay the return postage. Email us and we will tell you how to send the piece back.

We refund everything you paid, including the standard delivery cost, within 14 days of hearing from you. We may wait until the piece is back with us, or until you send proof that it is on its way, whichever comes first. The refund goes back the same way you paid.

Please pack it so it travels safely. If it comes back worn or damaged beyond what looking it over would cause, we may take that loss in value off the refund.

Items made to your own specification or clearly personalised are exempt from the 14-day right of withdrawal under the EU Consumer Rights Directive. In plain terms: a piece made at your request in a colour outside our usual range, or with a name printed on it, cannot be returned because you changed your mind. That exception never covers a piece that arrives damaged or faulty — tell us and we will put it right.

Delivery

Every order is printed after it is placed. Allow 3–5 working days in the workshop, then normal postal time. You get a tracking number on the day it ships.

Privacy

We set no cookies of our own and run no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. Our fonts and scripts are served from this site, not loaded from Google or other third parties.

Like any website, pages reach you through service providers who handle technical data, such as your IP address, in order to deliver them. This site is hosted by GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare, which also protects it from abuse. Cloudflare may set a strictly necessary security cookie to tell visitors from bots.

If you email us, we keep your message to answer it.
