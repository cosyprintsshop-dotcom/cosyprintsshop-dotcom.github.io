Why these

Made slowly, on purpose.

Light you can sit next to

The shades are printed in fine ridges, so the bulb reads as a soft even glow instead of a hard point. It is the difference between a room being lit and a room feeling warm.

Made once you ask

Nothing is mass-produced ahead of time. That means no dead stock, no landfill, and a finish choice made for your room rather than for a shipping container.

Plant-based plastic

Everything is printed in PLA, made from corn starch rather than crude oil. It is rigid, matte, and does not yellow the way cheap ABS does.

Repairable, not disposable

If a foot snaps or a base cracks, message us. We keep the files, so we can reprint the part instead of replacing the object.

How it works

Four steps, about a week.

Nothing is stocked. That is slower than a warehouse and it is the reason nothing gets thrown away.

  1. You order

    Pick a piece and a finish. Nothing is sitting in a warehouse, so we start yours once the order lands.

  2. We print it

    One machine, one piece at a time, in our workshop. A lamp takes most of a day; smaller objects a few hours.

  3. We finish it by hand

    Supports removed, seams cleaned, base checked flat. If it does not look right, it does not get boxed.

  4. It ships

    Packed in recycled card and posted within 3–5 working days. You get a tracking number the day it leaves.

Questions

The things people ask before ordering.

How long does an order take to arrive?
We print your order after it comes in, so allow 3–5 working days in the workshop plus normal postal time. France is usually 2 days after dispatch, the rest of Europe 4 to 6. You get tracking when it ships.
What is PLA, and is it any good?
PLA is a rigid plastic made from corn starch instead of oil. It holds fine detail, has a matte finish, and does not smell. It softens above roughly 55°C, so keep pieces out of direct summer sun on a windowsill and out of the dishwasher.
Can I get a piece in a different colour?
Usually, yes. The colours listed are what we stock, but if you want a lamp base to match something in your room, email us before ordering and we will tell you honestly whether we can get the filament.
Are the lamps safe to leave on?
They are built for LED bulbs, which run cool. Do not fit a halogen or incandescent bulb — the shade is plastic and is not rated for that heat. The bulb is not included unless the listing says so.
Can I return something?
Yes. You have 14 days from delivery to change your mind, no explanation needed, and we refund the item. The exception is anything we made to your own specification — a custom colour or a name printed on it — which we cannot resell.
What exactly happens with the 10%?
Ten percent of revenue — not profit, so it is paid whether or not we make money that month — goes to our local hospital. We publish what we sent and when, once a quarter.
Do you take custom commissions?
Small ones. If you want an existing piece resized or a simple object made, email us with rough dimensions. We will say no if it is not something we can print well.
