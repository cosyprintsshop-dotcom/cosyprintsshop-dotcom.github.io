Terms, returns and privacy

Returns and the right to change your mind

You have 14 days from the day you receive an order to withdraw from it, without giving a reason. Tell us by email and send the item back in a condition we can resell. We refund the item once it arrives.

Made-to-measure and personalised items are exempt from the 14-day right of withdrawal under the EU Consumer Rights Directive. In plain terms: a piece printed in a colour you chose specially, or with a name on it, cannot be returned.

Delivery

Every order is printed after it is placed. Allow 3–5 working days in the workshop, then normal postal time. You get a tracking number on the day it ships.

Privacy

We set no cookies of our own and run no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. Our fonts and scripts are served from this site, not loaded from Google or other third parties.

Like any website, pages reach you through service providers who handle technical data, such as your IP address, in order to deliver them. This site is hosted by GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare, which also protects it from abuse. Cloudflare may set a strictly necessary security cookie to tell visitors from bots.

If you email us, we keep your message to answer it.
