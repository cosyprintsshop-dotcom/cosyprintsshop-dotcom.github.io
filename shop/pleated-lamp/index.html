Lamps

Pleated Lamp

Sage & white

€59

The shade is four stacked cushions, each printed with a fine diamond texture. Switched off, it looks like stitched fabric. Switched on, the light leaks out brightest where the tiers meet, so the lamp glows in four bands rather than as one bright block. It sits on a rounded sage base with small feet. It is a mood lamp more than a reading lamp, and we would rather say so.

Order by email

Printed after you order. In the workshop 3–5 working days, then posted with tracking.

Material
PLA, plant-based
Light source
Warm-white LED, fitting being finalised
Heat limit
PLA softens above about 55 °C
Made
To order, in our workshop
Finish
Matte. Diamond-textured white shade, sage base

Wipe with a dry cloth. Keep out of direct summer sun and away from heat — PLA softens above about 55 °C. The texture holds dust, so a soft brush works better than a cloth on the shade.
