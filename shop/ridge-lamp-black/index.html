Ridge Lamp

Ink Black

€59

The shade is printed in fine vertical ridges, and that is the whole point of it. Light leaving a flat surface glares; light leaving a ridged one breaks up and lands soft. Sat on a bedside table it reads as a warm column rather than a bulb you can see. The base is a solid matte black cylinder that gives it enough weight to stay put.

Order by email

Printed after you order. In the workshop 3–5 working days, then posted with tracking.

Material

Finish

Bulb

Made
